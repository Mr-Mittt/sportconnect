# CLIENT-GROUP-SESSION-3 · Share a group session with members, and the group session notification texts

**Status:** `DONE` (2026-11-20)
**Type:** New Feature · **Depends on:** SESSION-GROUP-1, SESSION-GROUP-3
**Filed:** 2026-11-19, from the owner's group-session specification ("Modal: share button trigger for invitation dialog with group member only") and the notifications decided for SESSION-GROUP-1.

- **Share:** `canShare` in `SessionDetailModal` also allows a group session for a group member (today `session.groupId === null && session.isPublic`); `ShareSessionDialog` gets its list injected: for a group session the members come from `useGroupMembers` (one 100-row page, a known cap, noted) instead of friends; the Joined mark, the 50 limit and the sent/limit lines are unchanged. The `SESSION_SHARE_NOT_ALLOWED` text goes back to "a private session" (en/vi).
- **Notification types:** `session.group.created` ("{name} created {session} in {group}") and `session.group.cancelled` ("{name} cancelled {session}") added to the `NotificationType` union with a `getNotificationText` case each (the exhaustiveness guard fails the build otherwise), en/vi `enums.json`; the click opens the session (`entityType` SESSION).
- Strings en and vi; tests for the dialog in group mode, the two texts; stories; MSW share handler accepts a group session for members.

**Consumer census:** `canShare`, `ShareSessionDialog`/`useSessionDetailModalData` (`share` slice), `useShareSession`, `notificationText.ts`, `notifications/types.ts`, `errors.json`, `I18N_READINESS.md`, e2e mocks.

**Scope changes and decisions (owner, 2026-11-20, at the pickup):**
- The member list of the group Share dialog is **not a single 100-row page with a noted cap**: it **loads the first 100 members by default and has a "Load more" for the next page**. This replaces the ticket's "one 100-row page, a known cap". **The typed filter stays on the client, exactly like the friend list** (it filters the members loaded so far by name; a member on a page not loaded yet is reached with "Load more"). A **server-side search for both the friend list and the group member list** (the B23 member search, `GET /groups/{id}/members/search`, and a friend search endpoint if one is needed) is **out of scope here and filed as its own follow-up ticket** (owner, 2026-11-20, after first asking for member search in this ticket).
- The notification texts drop "in {group}": the notification carries no group id (server contract below), so they read "{name} created {session}" and "{name} cancelled {session}".
- The dialog's copy for a group session is its own (en and vi): title "Share with group members", "Search members", "No members found", instead of the friends wording.

---

**Server contract (built 2026-11-19; the whole contract is `modules/session/docs/GROUP_SESSION_DESIGN.md` § 4.3 and § 7).** `POST /api/sessions/{id}/shares` on a group session: any member of the group in any status (`403 SESSION_GROUP_MEMBER_REQUIRED` otherwise), `{ userIds }` of 1 to 50, answer `{ sent, skipped }`; the recipients are other members of the group (not friends), and anyone outside the group, deactivated, repeated or already in the session is skipped and counted. Notifications: `type` `session.group.created` (a group session was created or generated for you) and `session.group.cancelled` (a group session you were told about, or joined, was cancelled); each carries the session id and the actor and **no group id**. Add both `NotificationType` values, their `getNotificationText` cases and `enums.json` entries in en and vi.

## Implementation summary (DONE 2026-11-20)

**Design as approved, restated.** A group session is shareable from the detail's Share button by any member of its group (whoever can open the detail is one); the dialog lists the group's members, **the first 100 and a "Load more" for the next pages**, filtered by name **on the client** over what is loaded (exactly like the friend list; the API search for both lists is the follow-up pair `CLIENT-SESSION-53` + `U29`, filed). `SessionShareData` became kind-aware (`kind` `'friends' | 'group'`, `people: SharePerson[]`, `isPeopleLoading`, `isPeopleError`, `hasMore`, `isFetchingMore`, `onLoadMore`); `useSessionDetailModalData` loads friends for a standalone session and `useGroupMembersPages` (new, an infinite query over `GET /groups/{id}/members?page=&size=100`, its key under `groupMembers`) for a group one, only while the dialog is open, and never lists the caller; `ShareSessionDialog` takes `people`/`kind` with its own group copy (title "Share with group members", "Search members", "No members found.", the members error, "Load more members"); `canShare` is `groupId !== null || isPublic`. Two notification types, `session.group.created` and `session.group.cancelled`, with a `getNotificationText` case each ("{name} created {session}", "{name} cancelled {session}": **no "in {group}"**, the notification carries no group id) and en/vi `enums.json`; `SESSION_SHARE_NOT_ALLOWED` is back to naming a private session ("A private session can't be shared." / vi).

**What was built (beyond the list above).** `SharePerson` (types), `feedKeys.groupMembersPages`, `useGroupMembersPages` (+ test), the generalised dialog/modal/hook and their tests and stories (group states), the notification texts (+ en/vi tests); MSW: `GET /groups/:id/members` is a real Spring page (`page`, `size`) with a `groupMembersVolume` override (130 synthetic members for the owned group, `seedGroupMembersVolumeOnNextLoad`), `POST /sessions/:id/shares` accepts a group session for a member of its group (403 `SESSION_GROUP_MEMBER_REQUIRED` otherwise), skips ids that are not members of the owned group, and still refuses a private standalone session (403 `SESSION_SHARE_NOT_ALLOWED`); e2e in `session-share.spec.ts`.

**Divergences from the approved design.** Two, both at the owner's direction during planning: member search through the API was first asked for in this ticket and then moved out (client filter, follow-up `CLIENT-SESSION-53` and `U29`), and the notification text lost "in {group}". Known caveat kept as designed: the "Joined" mark covers only the players the modal loaded (the first 20), so a member beyond them looks shareable and the server skips and counts them.

**Consumer census (final).** `canShare` and `ShareSessionDialog`/`useSessionDetailModalData` (`share` slice): **updated here** with `SessionDetailModal.stories`, `SessionDetailModalShare.test`, `ShareSessionDialog.{test,stories}`, `useSessionDetailModalData.share.test`; `useShareSession`: compatible as-is (its comment still names friends; the request is the same); `notificationText.ts`, `notifications/types.ts` (the exhaustive union), `enums.json`, `errors.json`, `session.json` en/vi: updated here; `useGroupMembers` and its Members-tab consumers: compatible as-is (the mock's page size now follows `size`, which the tab sends as 100); e2e: the old "a group session has no Share button" test is replaced by "a private standalone session has no Share button" and two group tests.
