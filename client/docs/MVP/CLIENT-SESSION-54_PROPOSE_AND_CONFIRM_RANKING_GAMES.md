# CLIENT-SESSION-54 · Propose a game as a ranking game and confirm it

**Status:** `TODO`
**Type:** Feature · **Depends on:** SESSION-72
**Filed:** 2026-11-23, `/feature` session "statistic and ranking" (owner specification `modules/session/docs/GROUP_AND_REGION_STATISTIC_RANKING.md`; design `modules/session/docs/STATISTIC_RANKING_DESIGN.md`; BA hand-over `modules/session/docs/STATISTIC_RANKING_DELTA.md`).

In the session's Result section and the record dialog: each record shows its ranking state (`ranking.status` NORMAL, PROPOSED or VERIFIED, with who has confirmed, `ranking.confirmedBy`), a **Propose as ranking game** action for a participant who joined (a game can be proposed before it has a score, in a `SCHEDULED`, `ONGOING` or `COMPLETED` session within 24 hours of its end), and a **Confirm** action for a player named in a proposed record once its score is complete (a planned game with no score says "waiting for the score"). **No region is needed** to propose or confirm (owner, 2026-11-23: a game counts for the venue's region, not the player's), so there is no "Add region" dialog. The other players get a notification: new `NotificationType` `session.record.verification_requested` (three words) with its text and the route to the session (en and vi, CLIENT-NOTIF-4 rule), sent when a game is proposed and again when a proposed game is changed. Editing a record makes everyone's confirmation stale and the screen says so ("changed, confirm again"). Coded errors from SESSION-72 (`SESSION_RECORD_RANKING_PROPOSER_NOT_JOINED`, `SESSION_RECORD_RANKING_NOT_AVAILABLE`, `SESSION_RECORD_RANKING_NOT_RANKABLE` with its `reason`, `SESSION_RECORD_VERIFY_NOT_A_PLAYER`, `SESSION_RECORD_NOT_PROPOSED`, `SESSION_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`) get en and vi copy and an I18N-4 row; the `SESSION_RECORD_NOT_LOGGABLE` copy changes to "scheduled, ongoing or completed". The mirrored `SessionRecord` type gains `ranking`.

**Scope change (2026-11-25, SESSION-53 rework, owner):** a sport has a statistics and ranking switch (`statisticsEnabled` on the sport, off by default; the admin screen is SPORT-19). The server refuses to propose or confirm a ranking game for a sport that has it off (409 `SESSION_RECORD_RANKING_NOT_AVAILABLE`, already in this ticket's en/vi copy), so the **Propose as ranking game** and **Confirm** actions are hidden when the sport's `statisticsEnabled` is not true, and a game of such a sport shows no ranking state. The field is on the sport response the client already loads.

**Out of scope:** statistics screens (CLIENT-SESSION-41), a refusal action (not designed).

**Tests:** Vitest for the state display, the actions, the dialog and the notification text; mutation check; a scoped e2e with MSW. Visual baselines: derive at pickup (the Result section and record dialog).
