Single and Pair boards for the group (members), and for the player's region and country (everyone signed in), with the viewer's own row, the sport's columns, the "ranked at" time, and for the group owner or admin a **Refresh ranking** button that shows a countdown while the one-hour cooldown runs (429 `SESSION_RANKING_COOLDOWN`, `retryAfterSeconds`). A player with no region sees the country board and a prompt to add a region. en/vi, error copy and an I18N-4 row.

**Group member statistics (added 2026-11-24 by the SESSION-73 scope gate; CLIENT-SESSION-41 already said this screen arrives here).** A **Statistics** list for the group's members (one row per member: totals and the sport's columns, from `GET /api/sessions/group/{groupId}/stats`, totals only, paged; **no `sportId` is sent: the sport is the group's**, and a group with no sport answers 400 `SESSION_SPORT_REQUIRED`) and a **member detail** (tap a row: that member's totals, and their pair statistics, from `GET /api/sessions/group/{groupId}/stats/members/{userId}`, also with no `sportId`), sharing the table component of CLIENT-SESSION-41. A non-member sees the same refusal as the other group screens (403 `SESSION_GROUP_MEMBER_REQUIRED`). It **also depends on SESSION-73** (session backlog); the JSON shapes are in `documentation/md/STATISTICS_JSONB_REFERENCE.md`. en/vi for every string, and an I18N-4 row for the errors it can show. **Built by SESSION-73:** the list is `{columns, members: Page<{member, values}>, asOf}` (totals over all the group's games, no ranking split, no pair or partner data; page size 1 to 50, default 20); the detail is `{columns, member, totals, pairs, pairsTotal, partners, partnersTotal, asOf}` (a sport has one grouping, so one of the two lists is filled: badminton `partners`, football `pairs`; one page, `page` from 0 and `size` 1 to 50, the totals say how many exist; a member with no row has `null` values) and shows a member who left the group or was deactivated. Both answer one `403 SESSION_GROUP_MEMBER_REQUIRED` for a non-member or a missing group. Column labels are the client's, keyed by `key` (see CLIENT-SESSION-41).

## Built by SESSION-54 slice B (2026-11-26): the contract this screen reads (rewritten from the first guess)

Shapes: `documentation/md/STATISTICS_JSONB_REFERENCE.md` section 9.6. In short:
- **Boards:** `GET /api/sessions/group/{groupId}/leaderboard` (no `sportId`: the sport is the group's; members only, 403 `SESSION_GROUP_MEMBER_REQUIRED`), `GET /api/leaderboards/regions/{regionId}` and `/countries/{countryId}` (`sportId` required), each with `kind` `SINGLE` or `PARTNER`, `size` 1 to 50 and a **cursor** (send the previous `nextCursor`; **not** a page number, and a page can hold fewer than `size` entries while `nextCursor` is still set). The answer is `{sportId, scopeType, scopeId, kind, sort, total, entries, me, nextCursor, rankedAt}`; an entry is `{rank, prevRank, players, values}`: `rank` is shared by equal entries (1, 1, 3), `prevRank` is null for a new entry, `players` is one player or a pair, `values` are the sort values **in the order of `sort`** (the board shows only the stats that are in its sort list; the columns of the board are `sort`, not the sport's `columns`). `me` is the caller's best place (null when not listed); `rankedAt` null means the board was never built: show the empty state.
- **Errors to localize (en/vi):** `SESSION_RANKING_COOLDOWN` (429, `retryAfterSeconds`: the refresh countdown), `SESSION_RANKING_IN_PROGRESS` (409, try again shortly), `SESSION_GROUP_STATISTICS_OFF` (409, the group's switch is off), `SESSION_GROUP_ADMIN_REQUIRED` (403, refresh by a plain member), `SPORT_NOT_FOUND` (404: also while the sport's statistics are off), `SESSION_RANKING_KIND_INVALID` (400, not produced by the client).
- **Refresh:** `POST /api/sessions/group/{groupId}/leaderboard/refresh` (owner or admin) answers `{rankedAt, nextRefreshAt}`; show the button only to an owner or admin and count down to `nextRefreshAt`, or from `retryAfterSeconds` after a 429. The nightly build also resets the cooldown (`rankedAt` is the clock).
- **The group's own order (new, owner/admin):** `GET / PUT / DELETE /api/sessions/group/{groupId}/leaderboard/rules` (`PUT` body `{kind, sort:[{key,dir}|{sum,dir}], require:[{key,op,value}]}`; the keys are the sport's collected stats; the rule applies at the next build, which starts soon after the save). This screen's earlier "ranking-order settings" stay out of scope: a group edits its sort list in a later settings screen, or here if the owner asks for it when this ticket is picked up.
- **Hidden at read time:** deactivated players (every board) and members who left (group board) are not in `entries`, so ranks can skip; `total` still counts them until the next build.
