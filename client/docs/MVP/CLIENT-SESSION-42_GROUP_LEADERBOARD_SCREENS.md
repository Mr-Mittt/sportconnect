# CLIENT-SESSION-42 · Boards: the group's, the region's and the country's

**Status:** `TODO`
**Type:** Feature · **Depends on:** SESSION-54 (and review gate 2), SESSION-73 (the member statistics list and detail), CLIENT-SESSION-41
**Filed:** 2026-11-12 as "Group leaderboard screens and the ranking-order settings"; **rewritten 2026-11-23** by the `/feature` session "statistic and ranking" (`modules/session/docs/STATISTIC_RANKING_DESIGN.md`). The ranking-order admin settings and the season picker are superseded (the order is the sport's, a group's own order and seasons are later).

Single and Pair boards for the group (members), and for the player's region and country (everyone signed in), with the viewer's own row, the sport's columns, the "ranked at" time, and for the group owner or admin a **Refresh ranking** button that shows a countdown while the one-hour cooldown runs (`STATS_RANKING_COOLDOWN`, `retryAfterSeconds`). A player with no region sees the country board and a prompt to add a region. en/vi, error copy and an I18N-4 row.

**Group member statistics (added 2026-11-24 by the SESSION-73 scope gate; CLIENT-SESSION-41 already said this screen arrives here).** A **Statistics** list for the group's members (one row per member: totals and the sport's columns, from `GET /api/sessions/group/{groupId}/stats`, totals only, paged; **no `sportId` is sent: the sport is the group's**, and a group with no sport answers 400 `SESSION_SPORT_REQUIRED`) and a **member detail** (tap a row: that member's totals, and their pair statistics, from `GET /api/sessions/group/{groupId}/stats/members/{userId}`, also with no `sportId`), sharing the table component of CLIENT-SESSION-41. A non-member sees the same refusal as the other group screens (403 `SESSION_GROUP_MEMBER_REQUIRED`). It **also depends on SESSION-73** (session backlog); the JSON shapes are in `documentation/md/STATISTICS_JSONB_REFERENCE.md`. en/vi for every string, and an I18N-4 row for the errors it can show. **Built by SESSION-73:** the list is `{columns, members: Page<{member, values}>, asOf}` (totals over all the group's games, no ranking split, no pair or partner data; page size 1 to 50, default 20); the detail is `{columns, member, totals, pairs, pairsTotal, partners, partnersTotal, asOf}` (a sport has one grouping, so one of the two lists is filled: badminton `partners`, football `pairs`; one page, `page` from 0 and `size` 1 to 50, the totals say how many exist; a member with no row has `null` values) and shows a member who left the group or was deactivated. Both answer one `403 SESSION_GROUP_MEMBER_REQUIRED` for a non-member or a missing group. Column labels are the client's, keyed by `key` (see CLIENT-SESSION-41).

**Tests:** Vitest and a scoped e2e.
