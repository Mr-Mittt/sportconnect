# SESSION-53 · Statistics engine: the queue of completed sessions, the record reader, the history and the counters

**Status:** `DONE` (2026-11-25: the rework of "Scope changes (2026-11-25)" below; the first build was `DONE` 2026-11-23; review gate 1 next)
**Type:** Feature · **Depends on:** SESSION-72, LOC-5 (venue region, for the region and country counters) · **Review gate 1 follows this ticket** (data model and queue).
**Filed:** 2026-11-12 as "Result statistics (BA ST-1 to ST-7)"; **rewritten 2026-11-23** by the `/feature` session "statistic and ranking" (`modules/session/docs/STATISTIC_RANKING_DESIGN.md`). The earlier text (group-only statistics computed on read) is superseded.

Once a session is COMPLETED and its 24-hour guard (`scheduledEndAt + 24h`) has passed, the server reads its records, ignores the invalid ones, saves the counted games as history and updates the counters. Design sections 3, 4 and 6.

- **Generic by design (owner, 2026-11-23):** a `RecordTypeReader` per `record.data.type` turns a record into neutral facts (`valid`, `rankable`, participants with outcome and numeric metrics, teams); `COMPETITIVE` v1 is the only reader built (two teams, player counts within `playersPerSide`, UUIDs, no player twice, complete scores, outcome by position, metrics `scoreFor` and `scoreAgainst`, rankable when the first team's first-slot score is strictly higher). Everything after the reader (history, counters, verification, boards) sees only the facts. New optional schema blocks `record.statistics` (`metrics` with SUM, MAX or MIN, `derived` formulas, `counters`, `groupBy` of `PARTNER` and `TEAM`) and `record.ranking` (`sort`, `minGames` per `single` and `combo`), with badminton defaults when absent; written into `RECORD_SCHEMA_CONTRACT.md` and the badminton example. A shared fixture of cases per reader is run by the client's Vitest and the server's Spock; the fixtures include a second, football-like schema (goals and assists, no combos) so the generic paths are exercised.
- **Queue:** `session_stats_jobs`; a 5-minute `fixedDelay` tick with an `initialDelay` and no separate startup run (**scope change, owner, 2026-11-23: was a 60-second tick plus a startup run; the 24-hour guard makes a faster poll pointless, and fewer scheduled loops**); at most 20 sessions per tick, oldest guard first; claim by unique insert with skip; a stale `RUNNING` claim (10 minutes) is taken over by compare-and-set; statuses DONE, SKIPPED, RETRY (`attempts` and `next_attempt_at`, a fixed backoff, **scope change 2026-11-23: was the 1, 5, 15, 60 minute ladder**), FAILED after the last attempt (picked up again by the same tick after a long wait, **scope change: was revived at startup**); one `REQUIRES_NEW` transaction per session. Counting writes the `leaderboard_entry` rows and marks boards dirty during the day; ranking stays the nightly job of SESSION-54, which ranks only the dirty boards (**owner, 2026-11-23: counting every 5 minutes, ranking nightly**).
- **History:** pointers `{recordId, sessionId, ranking}` in the blobs, newest first, capped at 500 per player and sport; what each session contributed (the counted records with their ranking mark, ignored records with a reason) is kept once in `session_stats_jobs.result`, the permanent trail a rebuild reads (with `session_records` and the confirmations).
- **Statistics blobs (owner, 2026-11-23; session-owned tables):** `user_sport_stats (user_id, sport_id, personal, groupings, scopes, history, version)` and `group_member_stats (group_id, user_id, sport_id, stats, history, version)`. Core counters `games`, `wins`, `losses`, `draws` plus the sport's metrics; `teams` keyed by the sorted member ids of a combo (size from the schema); `scopes` one entry per venue region and country where the player earned ranking games (the venue's region and country, for every player of the game wherever they live; a combo counts there as a whole; owner, 2026-11-23, SESSION-72 pickup: no player-region comparison and no snapshot). Per counted game, in the session's one transaction: ensure the rows exist, lock them in sorted `user_id` order (`FOR UPDATE`), change the blobs in code, upsert the `leaderboard_entry` rows of the touched boards (`k1..k3` from `record.ranking.sort`, at most three keys), mark the boards dirty in `stat_board_runs`.
- **Imported and old sessions** are counted by the same tick (normal games).
- **Account lifecycle:** a background job; it counts deactivated players' games (history keeps them), boards hide them at read time (SESSION-54).
- **Census at pickup:** `LocationService`/`LocationResponse` (LOC-5 fields), `SportService.getResultSchema`, `SessionImportWriter`, the schema contract, `session-impl/CLAUDE.md`, `REDIS_KEYS.md` (no key added), ports (none).

**Out of scope:** a second record type's reader (SESSION-58), draws (SESSION-59), read endpoints (SESSION-73), ranks and boards (SESSION-54), head-to-head (SESSION-76), seasons (SESSION-55), guests (SESSION-52: a record with a guest is ignored with the reason `GUEST`).

**Tests (blobs):** the lock order and the upserts are PostgreSQL behaviour: a real-Postgres test of two sessions sharing players counted in parallel (no lost update, no deadlock), or listed as not run.

**Tests:** Spock for the reader (valid, each invalid shape, ranking tie), the counter maths for singles, pairs, group and standalone, a game at a venue with a region (counted for every player, whatever their own region) and at a venue with none (no regional counter, a logged reason), and the job statuses; `server/src/test/.../integration` for a real completed session end to end (counters and history written, a rerun counts nothing twice, an invalid record ignored with its reason, a session without a schema SKIPPED); real-Postgres concurrency (two workers on one session, parallel sessions touching the same player) or listed as not run; mutation check per rule.

## Scope changes (2026-11-23, owner, at the pickup scope gate)

- **A 5-minute tick, no start-up run, a simple retry** (was a 60-second tick plus a start-up run and the 1, 5, 15, 60 minute ladder): the 24-hour guard makes a faster poll pointless and fewer scheduled loops is better. Ranking stays nightly (SESSION-54); counting every 5 minutes only marks the touched boards dirty.
- **Two copies of the worker pattern, not a shared class** (owner): `SessionStatsWorker` copies `GroupSessionGenerationWorker`'s shape (one dedicated thread, a queue of one with a discard policy) without touching the generation code.
- **The shared fixture runs on the server only for now** (owner): the client's Vitest side is filed as **CLIENT-SESSION-58** (client backlog), so this server ticket does not trigger the client's full e2e run.
- **Grouping inside `record.statistics` (owner, 2026-11-24, after review of the blob layout):** the sport's schema declares `counters` (default `games`, `wins`) and `groupBy`, a list of dimensions that each get their own counters beside the totals: `PARTNER` (per teammate: A's games and wins with B) and `TEAM` with a `size` (today's combos, the only dimension that becomes a leaderboard entry). `combos` is replaced by `groupBy TEAM` (unmerged, nothing saved uses it). **The default is no `groupBy`** (owner, 2026-11-24): a sport asks for partner or team counters by declaring `groupBy`, so badminton's stored schema must declare it (there is no seed: the admin editor writes the schema). The blob key and column `teams` become `groupings` (`{PARTNER: {partnerId: ...}, TEAM: {comboKey: ...}}`), in `user_sport_stats`, in each `scopes` entry and in `group_member_stats.stats`; `all` and `ranked` keep their names and meaning. `V104` was edited in place at that point (the first build had not merged yet); it then merged unchanged as PR #474, so the 2026-11-25 rework below leaves it exactly as merged and adds `V105`. All jsonb shapes of the statistics tables are documented in one place, `documentation/md/STATISTICS_JSONB_REFERENCE.md`, for the client and later tickets.
- **Filed alongside:** **C14** (common backlog: the outbox relay retries a failing row every 10 seconds for ever, with no backoff, cap or signal; found while writing `documentation/md/BACKGROUND_JOBS.md`, which is new in this change with its `CLAUDE.md` rule).

## Scope changes (2026-11-25, owner, REWORK: the ticket is reopened after the first build merged as PR #474; `V104` stays exactly as merged and the new table and column are the migration `V105`)

After reviewing the engine (this ticket) and the read API (SESSION-73) the owner changed the data model. The working draft with the measurements is `modules/session/docs/STATISTICS_CONFIG_PAIRS_AND_H2H_DESIGN_DRAFT.md` (its "option A", pair and head-to-head tables as rows, was **rejected**: fixed counter columns do not fit a sport-defined stat list). The decisions below replace the earlier bullets wherever they differ.

1. **One config, shared with the client: `record.scoreboard.columns`.** The server collects exactly what the sport's columns need (`winRate` needs games and wins, so those are kept even when not listed). **No defaults at all**: no `record.scoreboard.columns`, or an empty list, means nothing is collected or calculated. **No fixed core counters** (`games`, `wins`, `losses`, `draws` are catalog stats like any other), **no custom `metrics` list, no `record.statistics` block and no badminton defaults**: a sport with running or swimming has no wins. `record.ranking` (`sort`, `minGames`) stays as the ranking order. `scoreboard.enabled` keeps its client meaning (hide the in-session table).
2. **A catalog of supported stats per record type** (`COMPETITIVE` now: `games`, `wins`, `losses`, `winRate`, `winDiff`, `scoreFor`, `scoreAgainst`, `scoreDiff`; a metric type later, SESSION-58). The client's `record-ui-v1.json` stays the source; the server keeps a copy guarded by a contract test, as for the reader fixture. The client's "show games, wins, losses when `columns` is missing" default goes (client ticket to file).
3. **A sport flag, `sports.statistics_enabled BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT FALSE`** (sport module; read through `sport-api`): statistics and ranking on or off together. Logging keeps its one implicit switch (a result schema on an active sport). The runner picks only sessions of enabled sports (a disabled sport gets no job row; switching it on makes the old sessions due), the processor checks again, and SESSION-72's `requestRanking`/`verify` refuse a disabled sport (`SESSION_RECORD_RANKING_NOT_AVAILABLE`). The admin editor switch and the census of every `Sport` consumer are part of this rework; the client part is a ticket to file.
4. **`groupBy` moves to `record.scoreboard.groupBy`, one grouping only, in the form the engine reads today**: a list of entries `{by: PARTNER}` or `{by: TEAM, size: n >= 2}`, the first usable entry kept. **Missing means no grouping at all**: nothing is stored under `groupings`, and no group head-to-head is built.
5. **Statistics stay in blobs, pairs included (owner: pair statistics are personal data; the mirrored copy, A with B in A's row and B with A in B's, is accepted).** `user_sport_stats` keeps `personal {all, ranked}`, `groupings`, `scopes` and `history`; `group_member_stats.stats` keeps plain `{counters, groupings}` with **no ranked split**. The counters inside them are only the collected columns.
6. **New table `group_stats (group_id, sport_id, h2h jsonb, version)`: the group's head-to-head**, written by the same counting transaction for a group session of a sport grouped by `PARTNER` (a sport with a `TEAM` grouping or none builds none). The blob is self-contained: a `members` dictionary (`userId` to a small number, never reused, kept for members who leave) and an `h2h` map whose key is the matchup `"a.b-c.d"` (a pair is its two member numbers, smaller first; the **smaller pair first** in the key; one tested helper does the ordering and the swap, rule A) with the value `[wins of the first pair, wins of the second, seq]`, and `[wins1, wins2, draws, seq]` for a sport that allows draws (the length tells which). `seq` is the group's running game number, kept in the blob; **the blob keeps the latest 5,000 matchups** (the least recently played entry is dropped when a new one would pass the cap), measured: about 11 bytes a matchup, a game costing 4 ms and 42 to 59 KB of log at 3,750 entries and 28 ms and 360 KB at 25,000. A game outside a group has no head-to-head.
7. **Draws: `record.data.allowDraws` (boolean, default false), defined in `RECORD_SCHEMA_CONTRACT.md` now.** The COMPETITIVE reader still refuses a game the first team did not win (`FIRST_TEAM_NOT_AHEAD`), so no game can be a draw end to end until SESSION-59; this ticket builds and unit-tests the head-to-head draw element, SESSION-59 makes the reader emit `DRAW`.
8. **The engine no longer writes `leaderboard_entry`** (owner): the table stays exactly as `V104` created it, and **SESSION-54's job builds and ranks every board** (group, region, country; singles and pairs) from the blobs. The engine keeps marking the touched boards dirty in `stat_board_runs`. SESSION-54 inherits the question of how to find "every player with an entry in this region" (a jsonb array has no index: a small index table or a GIN index).
9. **Consequences for the chain:** SESSION-73 is rebased on this and its reads follow the config (no `record.statistics`, pairs and partners paged from the blob); SESSION-76 reads `group_stats.h2h`; SESSION-77's rebuild reads the new columns and fills `group_stats`; a column added to a sport later has no history until that rebuild; SESSION-59 owns the reader's draw. Client tickets and the admin switch are filed after the duplicate check (see the backlog).

Design review answers for the new table: **table or column**: written once per group game, read as a whole, never joined or queried by an id across groups, so it could be a column of a group row, but the group module owns `groups` (cross-domain), hence a session-owned table; **two at once**: two sessions of one group counted together take the row lock on the `group_stats` row (created first, locked in the same ordered pass, group rows after user rows); **what the tests can prove**: the map maths, the cap and the key order on H2; the lock behaviour and the jsonb size on PostgreSQL only (listed as not run unless run).

## Implementation summary (2026-11-23, the FIRST build; superseded where the rework summary above says so)

**Built (session-impl, package `stats`; migration `V104`).**

- **Queue and claims.** `SessionStatsDrainJob` (`@Scheduled`, 5 minutes, first one after 1 minute, `app.stats.job.enabled`) only nudges `SessionStatsWorker` (thread `session-stats`, `app.stats.job.async`); `SessionStatsRunner` drains in passes of 50 until nothing is due or 10 minutes are spent: due job rows first (`RETRY` or `FAILED` whose time has come, a `RUNNING` claim older than 10 minutes), then `COMPLETED` sessions with no row whose `COALESCE(scheduledEndAt, scheduledStart) <= now - 24h`. A session with no row is claimed by a plain native `INSERT` (the primary key makes the loser fail, absorbed); a due row by the compare-and-set `claimDue`; the processor then locks the job row and checks it still owns the claim.
- **Processing** (`SessionStatsProcessor`, one `REQUIRES_NEW` transaction, all or nothing): reads the schema, records, confirmations, who is `JOINED` and the venue (`LocationService.getLocationsByIds`); each record through its reader; a record is counted when it is valid, has a complete score with the first team ahead and every named player is still `JOINED`; otherwise it is ignored with a reason kept in `session_stats_jobs.result` (`{counted: [{recordId, ranking}], ignored: [{recordId, reason}], notes}`, `ranking` being the same mark as in a history pointer). A counted game is a ranking game when proposed and confirmed by every named player at its current revision. It updates `user_sport_stats` (personal all and ranked, partners and pairs, the venue's REGION and COUNTRY scopes for a ranking game, history pointers capped at 500) and `group_member_stats` (a group session), upserts one `leaderboard_entry` per touched board entry from the session's final counters (`k1..k3` from the sport's sort keys, at most three) and marks the boards dirty in `stat_board_runs`.
- **Failure.** The whole session rolls back; `SessionStatsWriter.recordFailure` (own transaction, compare-and-set on the claim) sets `RETRY` 15 minutes ahead, `FAILED` after the fourth attempt (due again after 6 hours, one attempt each time).
- **Reader.** `RecordFacts` gains `teams` and `participants` (outcome, `scoreFor`, `scoreAgainst`); the short constructor SESSION-72 uses is kept. `StatisticsSchema`, `StatsFormulas`, `StatsCounters`, `StatsCombos`, `StatsHistory`, `PlayerStatsDoc` and `GroupStatsDoc` hold the pure logic and the only copy of the blob shapes.
- **Docs.** `BACKGROUND_JOBS.md` (new) and its `CLAUDE.md` rule; `RECORD_SCHEMA_CONTRACT.md` (the planned section is now the contract); design sections 3, 6 and 10; the BA delta; `session-impl/CLAUDE.md` rule 22; release notes `SERVER.md`; the deployment guide history row; hand-over notes in SESSION-73 and SESSION-54.

**Where it differs from the approved design** (said plainly, not silently updated):

1. A pass is 50 sessions and the worker loops for up to 10 minutes (the plan said 20 per tick, which would take days to drain an old backlog).
2. The claim is a plain `INSERT`, not `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (H2 does not run it). The first build used `save`, which **merges** onto an existing row: with four sessions counted by three threads, three jobs ended `SKIPPED` / `ALREADY_COUNTED` because a second worker overwrote the first one's claim. `SessionStatsIntegrationTest` found it; the second line of defence (the history pointer) is what stopped a double count.
3. Lock order: the missing rows are created first (each in its own `REQUIRES_NEW` transaction), then all rows of a table are locked in one ordered statement (user rows, then group-member rows), and boards are marked dirty last in sorted order. The design said "insert, then lock in sorted order", which still leaves a window where a row created after another was locked is locked in the opposite order by another session.
4. A game is counted only when the reader calls it `rankable`; a scored game the first team did not win is ignored (`FIRST_TEAM_NOT_AHEAD`) until draws exist (SESSION-59).
5. The football-like fixture schema has MAX and MIN metrics of the **team's** goals, a derived goal difference and no `groupBy`: the COMPETITIVE reader carries team scores only, so "goals and assists per player" is not exercisable until a reader gives per-player numbers.
6. `sport-impl` is not changed: more than three sort keys are dropped when the schema is read, not refused when it is saved.
7. `FAILED` is revived by the same tick after 6 hours, not by a start-up run; `subject_key` is `VARCHAR(255)` (a trio of ids does not fit 100).
8. The shared fixture is run by the server only (CLIENT-SESSION-58 for the client).
