# SESSION-53 · Statistics engine: the queue of completed sessions, the record reader, the history and the counters

**Status:** `TODO`
**Type:** Feature · **Depends on:** SESSION-72, LOC-5 (venue region, for the region and country counters) · **Review gate 1 follows this ticket** (data model and queue).
**Filed:** 2026-11-12 as "Result statistics (BA ST-1 to ST-7)"; **rewritten 2026-11-23** by the `/feature` session "statistic and ranking" (`modules/session/docs/STATISTIC_RANKING_DESIGN.md`). The earlier text (group-only statistics computed on read) is superseded.

Once a session is COMPLETED and its 24-hour guard (`scheduledEndAt + 24h`) has passed, the server reads its records, ignores the invalid ones, saves the counted games as history and updates the counters. Design sections 3, 4 and 6.

- **Generic by design (owner, 2026-11-23):** a `RecordTypeReader` per `record.data.type` turns a record into neutral facts (`valid`, `rankable`, participants with outcome and numeric metrics, teams); `COMPETITIVE` v1 is the only reader built (two teams, player counts within `playersPerSide`, UUIDs, no player twice, complete scores, outcome by position, metrics `scoreFor` and `scoreAgainst`, rankable when the first team's first-slot score is strictly higher). Everything after the reader (history, counters, verification, boards) sees only the facts. New optional schema blocks `record.statistics` (`metrics` with SUM, MAX or MIN, `derived` formulas, `combos.size`) and `record.ranking` (`sort`, `minGames` per `single` and `combo`), with badminton defaults when absent; written into `RECORD_SCHEMA_CONTRACT.md` and the badminton example. A shared fixture of cases per reader is run by the client's Vitest and the server's Spock; the fixtures include a second, football-like schema (goals and assists, no combos) so the generic paths are exercised.
- **Queue:** `session_stats_jobs`; a 60-second tick and a startup run; at most 20 sessions per tick, oldest guard first; claim by unique insert with skip; a stale `RUNNING` claim (10 minutes) is taken over by compare-and-set; statuses DONE, SKIPPED, RETRY (1, 5, 15, 60 minutes), FAILED (revived at startup); one `REQUIRES_NEW` transaction per session.
- **History:** pointers `{recordId, sessionId, ranking}` in the blobs, newest first, capped at 500 per player and sport; what each session contributed (counted record ids, ignored records with a reason) is kept once in `session_stats_jobs.result`, the permanent trail a rebuild reads (with `session_records` and the confirmations).
- **Statistics blobs (owner, 2026-11-23; session-owned tables):** `user_sport_stats (user_id, sport_id, personal, teams, scopes, history, version)` and `group_member_stats (group_id, user_id, sport_id, stats, history, version)`. Core counters `games`, `wins`, `losses`, `draws` plus the sport's metrics; `teams` keyed by the sorted member ids of a combo (size from the schema); `scopes` one entry per venue region and country where the player earned ranking games (their snapshot matches the venue; a combo counts only when every member matches). Per counted game, in the session's one transaction: ensure the rows exist, lock them in sorted `user_id` order (`FOR UPDATE`), change the blobs in code, upsert the `leaderboard_entry` rows of the touched boards (`k1..k3` from `record.ranking.sort`, at most three keys), mark the boards dirty in `stat_board_runs`.
- **Imported and old sessions** are counted by the same tick (normal games).
- **Account lifecycle:** a background job; it counts deactivated players' games (history keeps them), boards hide them at read time (SESSION-54).
- **Census at pickup:** `LocationService`/`LocationResponse` (LOC-5 fields), `SportService.getResultSchema`, `SessionImportWriter`, the schema contract, `session-impl/CLAUDE.md`, `REDIS_KEYS.md` (no key added), ports (none).

**Out of scope:** a second record type's reader (SESSION-58), draws (SESSION-59), read endpoints (SESSION-73), ranks and boards (SESSION-54), head-to-head (SESSION-76), seasons (SESSION-55), guests (SESSION-52: a record with a guest is ignored with the reason `GUEST`).

**Tests (blobs):** the lock order and the upserts are PostgreSQL behaviour: a real-Postgres test of two sessions sharing players counted in parallel (no lost update, no deadlock), or listed as not run.

**Tests:** Spock for the reader (valid, each invalid shape, ranking tie), the counter maths for singles, pairs, group and standalone, region match and mismatch, and the job statuses; `server/src/test/.../integration` for a real completed session end to end (counters and history written, a rerun counts nothing twice, an invalid record ignored with its reason, a session without a schema SKIPPED); real-Postgres concurrency (two workers on one session, parallel sessions touching the same player) or listed as not run; mutation check per rule.
