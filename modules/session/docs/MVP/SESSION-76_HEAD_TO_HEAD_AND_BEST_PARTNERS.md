# SESSION-76 · Head-to-head, best partners and the opponent a player loses to most (BA ST-2, ST-6)

**Status:** `TODO`
**Type:** Feature · **Depends on:** SESSION-53
**Filed:** 2026-11-23, `/feature` session "statistic and ranking" (owner specification `modules/session/docs/GROUP_AND_REGION_STATISTIC_RANKING.md`; design `modules/session/docs/STATISTIC_RANKING_DESIGN.md`; BA hand-over `modules/session/docs/STATISTIC_RANKING_DELTA.md`). The owner's specification does not mention head-to-head, but the BA lists it as Must (ST-2); it is not in the first statistics release (STATISTIC_RANKING_DELTA section 4, question 5), so it is filed as its own ticket.

Read at request time from the player's history pointers (the latest 500 games, resolved by id from `session_records`) and the `groupings` of `user_sport_stats` (SESSION-53, `documentation/md/STATISTICS_JSONB_REFERENCE.md`): for two players, as opponents (who won how many games; there is no `OPPONENT` grouping, so this is read from the history) and as partners (games and wins together: already stored under `groupings.PARTNER.<partnerId>`, all and ranked, **for a sport grouped by `PARTNER`** such as badminton, so no history read is needed for it; a sport grouped by `TEAM` has `groupings.TEAM` instead, and a sport with no `groupBy` has neither, so this read falls back to the history); a player's most frequent and best partners and the opponent they lose to most. A cached opponent map inside the blob is a later option if the read is too slow (measure at pickup). Group scope for group members, personal otherwise; visibility follows SESSION-74 when it exists. Client pair: CLIENT-SESSION-56.

**Tests:** Spock against a worked example; an integration test for the visibility.

## Hand-over from the SESSION-53 rework (2026-11-25, owner)

- **Head-to-head is the `group_stats.h2h` document, not the history.** One document per group and sport, built only for a **group session of a `PARTNER` sport** and games of **exactly two pairs** (`GroupH2hDoc`; shape in `documentation/md/STATISTICS_JSONB_REFERENCE.md` section 5): a `members` dictionary (user id to a small number) and a map `"a.b-c.d": [wins of the first pair, wins of the second, seq]` (`[w1, w2, draws, seq]` when `record.data.allowDraws`), the smaller pair first in the key. To read the head-to-head of two pairs, map the four user ids to their numbers, order each pair, order the two pairs, and look up the key (`GroupH2hDoc.matchup`); the wins follow the key order. Only the latest 5,000 matchups of a group are kept, so an old matchup may be gone.
- **Not stored, so not available from it:** a head-to-head outside a group, per region or country, and an individual A-against-X inside doubles. The text of this ticket that reads the history (the latest 500 pointers) for these is its fallback; decide at pickup whether they are wanted (they cost a history read, and "the opponent a player loses to most" can only come from there).
- **Best partners** come from `user_sport_stats.groupings.PARTNER` (and `group_member_stats.stats.groupings.PARTNER` for a group), a `PARTNER` sport only.
- The sport must have statistics switched on; a sport with the switch off answers as an unknown sport.
