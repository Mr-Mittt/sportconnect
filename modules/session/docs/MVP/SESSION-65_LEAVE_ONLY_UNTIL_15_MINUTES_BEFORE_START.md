# SESSION-65 · A joined participant can leave only until 15 minutes before the session starts

**Status:** `TODO`
**Type:** Enhancement (business rule)
**Depends on:** none (the client side is CLIENT-GL-51, which hides the Leave button after the cutoff and ships the hint; deploy this first)
**Filed:** 2026-11-17, owner decision while scoping CLIENT-GL-51 (`/workon client`): "You can ONLY leave the session up to 15 minutes before its start time."

`SessionServiceImpl.leaveSession` today lets a JOINED participant leave at any time, even during the session. New rule: a participant whose status is `JOINED` may leave only while `now <= scheduledStart - 15 minutes`. Later, the call is refused with a coded 409/400 (`SESSION_LEAVE_TOO_LATE`, C12 contract; `errorParams` carries the cutoff instant). The 15 minutes is a named constant (later a setting), and the client mirrors it.

**Unchanged on purpose:** declining an invitation (`INVITED` to `LEFT`) and cancelling a join request (`REQUESTED` to `LEFT`) are not leaving a place someone counted on, so they stay possible at any time (to confirm with the owner at pickup). The creator rules (`SESSION_CREATOR_CANNOT_LEAVE`) are untouched. A session still in `PREPARING` with no start has no cutoff.

**Open at pickup:** an `ONGOING` or `COMPLETED` session is past the cutoff, so a JOINED participant can no longer leave it (the owner's rule as stated; confirm). Whether group sessions generated by the job follow the same rule (assumed yes).

Client: en/vi copy for `SESSION_LEAVE_TOO_LATE` in `errors:codes` is part of CLIENT-GL-51. Account lifecycle: no new endpoint, the caller already passed the per-request gate. Notification use case: none.

**Tests:** Spock for the boundary (exactly 15 minutes before allowed, one second later refused), INVITED and REQUESTED unaffected, and an IT through the real pipeline for the status mapping.
