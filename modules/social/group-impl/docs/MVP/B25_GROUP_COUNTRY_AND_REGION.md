# B25 · A group has a country and a region (required, from its owner's)

**Status:** `TODO`
**Type:** New Feature · **Depends on:** none (the reference module already serves countries and regions)
**Filed:** 2026-11-27, owner decision at the pickup of SESSION-55 (group seasons): a group needs a country and a region, for the **local date** of its sessions (a season counts a session by its local date), and for the country rules of its ranking (the "group country later" of SESSION-54, where a group board uses the group's own rule or the sport's and cannot use a country's). Duplicate check: no group or client ticket names a group country or region (searched the group and client backlogs and the ticket folders).

A group has a required `countryId` and `regionId` (ids of the reference module, no foreign key, like every cross-domain reference): the owner's country and region are filled in when the group is created and the owner can change them; a region must belong to its country (checked through the reference `-api`). The group's response carries both. **Migration:** every existing group takes its owner's country and region; a group whose owner has none is **invalid business data and is removed from use**: the migration sets `is_active = false` on it (a soft delete: a hard delete inside a Liquibase migration would cascade to members, sessions and posts) and the number of such groups is logged and written in the release notes, so the owner can look at them before anything is purged. The constraint on active groups is decided at pickup (a partial constraint, or a placeholder).

**Open at pickup:** where the owner's country and region live (the user domain, the Keycloak attributes of A32); whether the owner can change them after creation; what happens to an active group when its owner's data is missing later; the Liquibase and H2 mirror steps; the consumers of `Group`, `CreateGroupRequest`, `GroupResponse` (census) and the client forms (CLIENT-GROUP-GEO-1).

**Out of scope:** a group's timezone as its own field (derived from the region), moving a group between countries with seasons in progress.

**Tests:** an IT for creation (defaults from the owner, a region of another country refused) and for the migration step (a group with no owner data is deactivated, the others filled); mutation-check each.

## Found 2026-11-27 (Phase 2 of SESSION-55): a country and a region do not give a timezone
The reference module's `Country` (iso2, iso3, name, default language) and `Region` (iso code, name) have no timezone, and a `Location`'s timezone is nullable. SESSION-55 needs the group's **local date**, so this ticket must also give the group a timezone: either a `default_timezone` on `countries` (and an optional `timezone` on `regions` for a country that spans several zones) seeded for the active countries, with the group's zone derived from its region, then country; or a `timezone` column on the group itself (an IANA id, initialised from the owner's region and country and editable by the owner). To decide at the pickup of B25.

## Decision 2026-11-27 (owner): why the group has its own country and region, and who sets them
A group's country and region are **chosen by its owner or an admin** (`canManageMembers`), not only copied from the owner: the owner's values are only the pre-fill at creation. They are wanted in their own right, not just for a timezone: later the app can **suggest locations for a group from its region** (the group's sessions and its regular-session configurations pick a venue, and today a venue has to be found from scratch), and a group's board can use the country's ranking rules. This settles the open point "whether the owner can change them after creation": the owner and an admin can, with the region checked against the country.

## Decision 2026-11-27 (owner): the group's timezone is derived from its region, then its country (option A)
The reference module gets the timezone: a `timezone` column (IANA id) on `countries`, seeded `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` for Vietnam, and an optional `timezone` on `regions` that overrides the country's (empty until a country that spans several zones is activated). **The group has no timezone field of its own**: the group response carries a derived `timezone` (its region's, else its country's); a country with no timezone fails clearly (no guess), so activating a country is an admin step that includes its zone. The reference migration, its seed and the `-api` DTOs (`CountryResponse`, `RegionResponse` with the zone) are part of this ticket. The group's zone is what SESSION-55 (seasons) uses for its local dates.
